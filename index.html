MAP Spring 26 Reporting Criteria

This dashboard shows both progress cycles together: Fall 25 → Spring 26 and Spring 25 → Spring 26. Blank cells and 0 values are ignored.
AreaRule
Spring 26 AttainmentBelow = percentile < 41; At Level = 41–60; Above Level = ≥61. At & Above = At + Above.
Progress MappingExpected or Better = No* + Yes* + Yes. Better than Expected = Yes* + Yes. No Progress = No only.
Progress Valid CountOnly students with a valid Yes, Yes*, No, or No* value are counted. Blank and 0 cells are excluded from the denominator.
Judgment Step 1If At & Above / Expected or Better is <15% = Very Weak; 15%–74% = Weak; ≥75% moves to Step 2.
Judgment Step 2Above / Better than Expected: <50% = Acceptable; 50%–60% = Good; 61%–74% = Very Good; ≥75% = Outstanding.
